# Cabin Analytics - Go-To-Market Strategy

## Value Proposition

**For website owners who want analytics without compromising visitor privacy**

Cabin provides simple, GDPR-compliant website analytics that respects your users. Unlike Google Analytics, we don't use cookies, don't track personal data, and don't slow down your site.

## Target Audience

### Primary
- Indie hackers and solopreneurs
- Privacy-conscious developers
- Small business owners
- Bloggers and content creators

### Secondary
- Marketing agencies (white-label potential)
- Enterprise compliance teams
- EU-based businesses (GDPR requirements)

## Key Differentiators

1. **Privacy by Design**: No cookies, no personal data, fully compliant
2. **Speed**: <1KB tracking script vs 40KB+ for GA
3. **Simplicity**: No overwhelming dashboards, just insights that matter
4. **Price**: Affordable at $9/mo vs $150+/mo for enterprise tools

## Pricing Strategy

**Free Tier**: Lead generation
- 1 website, 10K pageviews
- Goal: 1,000 free users → 5% conversion = 50 paid customers

**Pro Tier ($9/mo)**: Main revenue driver
- 5 websites, 100K pageviews
- Target: 400 customers = $3,600 MRR

**Business Tier ($29/mo)**: High-value customers
- Unlimited websites, 1M pageviews
- Target: 50 customers = $1,450 MRR

**Total Target**: $5,050 MRR

## Conversion Funnel

```
Landing Page Visitors (10,000/mo)
    ↓ 30% click "Get Started"
Sign Ups (3,000/mo)
    ↓ 50% add website
Active Users (1,500/mo)
    ↓ 10% hit free limit
Paying Customers (150/mo)
    ↓ 20% churn
Net New Customers (120/mo)
```
